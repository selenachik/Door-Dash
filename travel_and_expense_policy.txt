MERIDIAN FIXTURES INC.
TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Effective: January 1, 2026 | Policy Number: FIN-014 | Owner: Controller's Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs reimbursement of business travel and entertainment expenses for all
Meridian Fixtures employees. It supersedes all prior versions.

2. AIRFARE
2.1 Domestic flights under 5 hours: economy class only.
2.2 Domestic flights over 5 hours, or any international flight: economy plus or premium
    economy permitted with manager pre-approval. Business class requires VP-level approval
    and is limited to flights over 8 hours.
2.3 Airfare must be booked at least 14 days in advance except in documented emergency
    travel situations, which require Controller sign-off within 5 business days after booking.

3. LODGING
3.1 Standard nightly cap: $275/night domestic, $340/night for the following high-cost
    cities: New York, San Francisco, Boston, Chicago, Los Angeles, Seattle, Washington DC.
3.2 International lodging caps are set by destination in Appendix A (not reproduced here).
3.3 Any hotel booking exceeding the cap by more than 15% requires written manager approval
    BEFORE booking, not after.

4. GROUND TRANSPORTATION
4.1 Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) and taxi are reimbursable for business travel.
4.2 Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate,
    updated annually and posted on the Finance intranet page.
4.3 Rental cars require mid-size or smaller unless traveling with 3+ colleagues or
    transporting equipment; upgrades beyond that require manager approval.

5. MEALS
5.1 Per diem model: $75/day domestic, $95/day international, no receipts required
    under the per diem cap.
5.2 Employees may instead submit actual itemized receipts if they exceed the per diem,
    but ONLY with manager pre-approval for the specific trip; without pre-approval,
    reimbursement is capped at the standard per diem regardless of receipts submitted.
5.3 Alcohol is reimbursable only when entertaining a client, capped at $50/person, and
    must be itemized separately from the meal on the expense report.

6. BEREAVEMENT AND EMERGENCY TRAVEL
6.1 Employees traveling due to the death or serious illness of an immediate family member
    may book at any fare class necessary given short notice, subject to Controller
    post-approval within 5 business days.
6.2 Bereavement-related bookings are EXEMPT from the 14-day advance booking rule in 3.3.
6.3 There is no bereavement-specific lodging or meal cap increase; standard caps in
    Sections 3 and 5 still apply.

7. SUBMISSION AND DEADLINES
7.1 All expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the trip end date
    via the Concur expense system.
7.2 Reports submitted after 60 days require Controller approval and may be denied at
    the Controller's discretion.
7.3 Reports missing required receipts (any single item over $25) will be returned for
    correction, not auto-denied, unless resubmission also fails to include the receipt.

8. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS
The following are never reimbursable regardless of approval level: traffic and parking
fines, personal entertainment (movies, minibar, spa services), travel insurance for
personal trips, loss or theft of personal items, and airline change fees caused by
personal schedule changes (change fees due to a business schedule change ARE reimbursable
with a brief note in the expense report explaining the business reason).

9. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
Any exception to this policy requires written approval from the Controller. Verbal
approval from a manager is not sufficient for exceptions to Sections 2, 3, or 5.

END OF POLICY FIN-014
