MERIDIAN FIXTURES INC.
PROCUREMENT AND ACCOUNTS PAYABLE POLICY
Effective: March 1, 2026 | Policy Number: FIN-009 | Owner: Controller's Office

1. PURCHASE APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
1.1 Purchases under $1,000: department manager approval only.
1.2 Purchases $1,000-$9,999: department manager + Director approval.
1.3 Purchases $10,000-$49,999: Director + VP approval, and a minimum of two vendor
    quotes on file.
1.4 Purchases $50,000 and above: VP + CFO approval, minimum three vendor quotes, and
    Procurement committee review before a purchase order is issued.
1.5 Sole-source purchases (only one viable vendor) above $10,000 require a documented
    sole-source justification memo regardless of the quote requirement in 1.3/1.4.

2. NEW VENDOR SETUP
2.1 All new vendors must be entered in the vendor master file by Accounts Payable before
    any invoice can be processed against them.
2.2 New vendor setup requires a completed W-9 (domestic) or W-8BEN (foreign), a signed
    vendor agreement, and banking details verified by a callback to a phone number
    NOT provided in the new vendor's setup email (a fraud-prevention control — banking
    changes communicated only by email are not to be actioned without independent
    verification).
2.3 Vendor master file changes to existing vendor banking details require the same
    independent callback verification as new vendor setup, regardless of who requests
    the change or how urgent it is represented to be.

3. INVOICE PROCESSING
3.1 Standard payment terms are Net 30 from invoice date unless a different term is
    specified in the vendor agreement.
3.2 A three-way match (purchase order, receiving confirmation, and invoice) is required
    before payment for any invoice over $2,500. Invoices under $2,500 may be processed
    with a two-way match (PO and invoice only).
3.3 Invoices without a valid PO number will be routed back to the requesting department
    for a retroactive PO; Accounts Payable will not process a PO-required invoice without
    one, even with manager approval, except under the emergency purchase provision in 4.1.
3.4 Duplicate invoice numbers from the same vendor are automatically held by the AP
    system for manual review and are never auto-paid.

4. EMERGENCY PURCHASES
4.1 In a genuine operational emergency (e.g., equipment failure halting production), a
    department Director may authorize an emergency purchase without a prior PO, provided
    a retroactive PO and written justification are filed with Accounts Payable within
    3 business days.
4.2 Emergency purchase authority does NOT exempt the purchase from the approval
    thresholds in Section 1 — a $60,000 emergency purchase still requires CFO approval,
    just after the fact rather than before.

5. CREDIT CARD (P-CARD) PURCHASES
5.1 Individual P-Card transaction limit: $2,500 unless a higher limit is specifically
    approved by the CFO for a named cardholder.
5.2 Monthly P-Card statements must be reconciled and coded by the cardholder within
    10 business days of statement close.
5.3 P-Cards may not be used for any purchase that would otherwise require the vendor
    setup process in Section 2 (i.e., cannot be used to bypass new vendor controls).

6. RECORD RETENTION
6.1 All purchase orders, invoices, and approval records must be retained for 7 years
    per Finance's document retention schedule, referenced separately in policy FIN-002.

END OF POLICY FIN-009
